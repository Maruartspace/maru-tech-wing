Roast — 208°C, until the first crack releases the aroma.
Crack — sugars caramelize, the shell splits open, the scent turns dark and sweet.
Grind — fresh, seconds before brewing. Never a minute before.
Brew — slow, in hand-hammered copper, over low heat, the old way.
Serve — poured into hand-thrown ceramic. The last step — and the only one you taste.
