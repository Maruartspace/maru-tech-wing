The Journey

From seed to cup, nothing hidden

Five moments, one bean. Every stage changes its aroma — we roast, grind and pour to protect exactly that.

01 — Roast

208°C, and the room already smells like this

Beans tumble slowly until the first crack releases their oils — and their aroma. We roast in batches small enough to taste every one.

02 — Crack

The moment it happens

Sugars caramelize, the shell splits open, and the scent turns from grassy to deep, dark and sweet. This is the bean we stop the roast at.

03 — Grind

Ground fresh, seconds before brewing

Never a minute before. Aroma fades fast once a bean is broken open — so nothing is pre-ground, ever.

04 — Brew

Still made the old way

Slow-brewed in hand-hammered copper over low heat, foam rising to the rim — filling the whole room with it before it ever reaches a cup.

05 — Serve

Poured into hand-thrown ceramic

The last step of a very long journey — and the only one you actually get to taste.
