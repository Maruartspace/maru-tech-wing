FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I care for my jewelry?

Take it off before showering, sleeping or working out. Store it in the pouch, away from other pieces, and polish with a soft cloth once a month.

How long does shipping take?

2–4 days within Spain, 7–14 days worldwide. Your tracking number arrives as soon as the order ships.

Can I return or exchange it?

Yes, within 30 days of delivery, as long as it's unworn and the packaging is intact.

Do you make custom pieces?

Yes. For a custom order, message us on Telegram — we'll talk stone, size and timeline.
